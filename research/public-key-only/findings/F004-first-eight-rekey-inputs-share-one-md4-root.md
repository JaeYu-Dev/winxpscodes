# F004 — V17 first-eight rekey inputs form two repeated-MD4 families, not eight independent inputs
Status: SP3 RAW-BLOB CONFIRMED
PK-only effect: strong dependency reduction in ADVAPI→KSecDD user-side inputs; not yet a whole-RNG break.

## Correction history
The original F004 draft proposed one MD4 root for all eight first-use rekey inputs. Direct comparison of the eight V17 raw 256-byte IOCTL input blobs falsifies that exact claim.

The actual pattern is more structured:

- `C1` is generated from one digest `D1` at offset 0.
- `C2..C8` are generated by repeatedly XOR-embedding a **second, identical digest D2** at offsets 7,14,21,28,35,42,49.

Thus the eight observed inputs are not independent 256-byte values; they lie in a family generated by at most two 128-bit MD4 roots for this trace.

## Raw values
For the first `SystemFunction036` pre-call buffer:

`B1 = 98 19 03 68 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 68 00 ae 25 00 00 00 00 00`

`D1 = MD4(B1) = 59ca1e7af9c27a9a3143d4ef1071d2f8`.

The raw `C1` blob is exactly:

`D1 || 0^240`.

For the second `SystemFunction036` pre-call buffer captured by V17:

`B2 = 10 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 70 4b 25 00 40 fb 23 00 08 00 00 00`

and:

`D2 = MD4(B2) = a01c025412d8ac6b22f983a68580bf56`.

Direct raw-blob comparison gives, for j=2..8:

`C_j XOR C_{j-1} = Embed_{7(j-1)}(D2)`

with exactly 16 nonzero bytes at each expected shifted position and zero differences elsewhere.

## Call-boundary interpretation
This pattern matches F003:
- call #1 hashes B1, rekeys entry1, and emits 20 bytes immediately because the NT rekey threshold mutates mid-call;
- call #2 hashes B2, then first-use rekeys entries2..0 with zero output; B2 remains unchanged across those seven passes, so the same D2 is injected at successive 7-byte offsets.

## Cardinality statement
For a fixed zero initial circular state and no interleaving, the **observed user-side first-eight rekey-input tuple** is a deterministic function of `(D1,D2)`:

`|(C1,...,C8) image| <= 2^256`.

That global bound is not itself below the ECDLP baseline. The more important dependency fact is that `C2..C8`, conditioned on prior C1, are all generated from a **single 128-bit D2** rather than seven independent inputs.

## Why this matters
The seven KSecDD rekeys during the second call do not introduce seven independent user-side seed roots. They receive deterministic snapshots of one repeated MD4 projection. Moreover, F005 shows those seven rekeys do not affect the second `SystemFunction036` output at all, because that output returns to the already-keyed entry1 stream.

## Remaining blocker
Kernel persistent state/pool evolution during the seven KSecDD rekeys can still be high-dimensional. F004 only eliminates independence on the ADVAPI caller-input side.
